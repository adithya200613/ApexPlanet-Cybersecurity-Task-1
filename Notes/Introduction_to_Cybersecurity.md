# Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity protects computers, networks, applications and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage and disruption.

## Why it matters
- Protects personal and organizational information.
- Reduces financial loss and service interruption.
- Supports privacy, trust and reliable digital services.

## Key terms
- **Asset:** Something valuable that needs protection.
- **Threat:** A possible cause of harm.
- **Vulnerability:** A weakness that could be exploited.
- **Risk:** The possibility and impact of a threat exploiting a vulnerability.
- **Exploit:** A method that takes advantage of a vulnerability.
- **Mitigation:** A measure that reduces risk.

## Common controls
Authentication, MFA, authorization, least privilege, firewalls, segmentation, patching, backups, logging and security awareness.

Only test systems you own or have explicit permission to assess.
