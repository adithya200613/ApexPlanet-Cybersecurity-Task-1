# Types of Cyber Threats

- **Malware:** Malicious software such as viruses, worms, Trojans, ransomware and spyware.
- **Phishing:** Deceptive messages or sites that trick users into revealing information or installing malware.
- **Password attacks:** Brute force, dictionary attacks, password spraying and credential stuffing.
- **Denial of Service:** Attempts to make a service unavailable by exhausting resources or disrupting communication.
- **Man-in-the-Middle:** Interception or manipulation of communication between parties.
- **Insider threat:** Risk caused by someone with legitimate access, intentionally or accidentally.
- **Social engineering:** Manipulating people into disclosing information or taking unsafe actions.
- **Web application threats:** Examples include injection, cross-site scripting, broken access control and insecure configuration.

## Prevention
Use MFA, unique passwords, updates, backups, access controls, user awareness and monitoring.
