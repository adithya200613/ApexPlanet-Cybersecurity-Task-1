# CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is a foundational information-security model.

## Confidentiality
Information is accessible only to authorized people or systems. Controls include encryption, passwords, MFA and access permissions.

## Integrity
Information remains accurate, complete and trustworthy. Controls include hashes, digital signatures, audit logs and change control.

## Availability
Systems and information remain accessible when required. Controls include backups, redundancy, maintenance and capacity planning.

| Principle | Main question | Example control |
|---|---|---|
| Confidentiality | Who can see it? | Encryption and access control |
| Integrity | Can we trust it? | Hashes and audit logs |
| Availability | Can we access it? | Backups and redundancy |
