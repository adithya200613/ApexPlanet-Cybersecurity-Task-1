# Attack Vectors

An attack vector is a route or method used to reach a target.

1. Exposed network services.
2. Weak or reused passwords.
3. Phishing links and attachments.
4. Unpatched software.
5. Misconfigured permissions or storage.
6. Infected removable media.
7. Compromised third-party software or suppliers.
8. Unauthorized physical access.

Reduce exposure by patching, disabling unnecessary services, applying least privilege, using MFA, segmenting networks and training users.

Metasploitable2 intentionally exposes vulnerable services for training. Keep it isolated and test it only from the authorized lab.
